# PRODUCT.md — kadabra

## Product
kadabra (always lowercase) is a Costa Rican studio that runs hands-on AI workshops for company teams. Typical unit: a half day on site or remote (discovery + hands-on with the team's real tasks), followed by a one-page written plan the next week. Scope is adapted per client. No software, no licences, no platform to sell.

## Primary user
Owner, general manager or operations lead of a Costa Rican company (10–300 people; services, finance, retail, logistics, professional firms). Reads English comfortably. Careful with money, allergic to corporate hype, contacts vendors on WhatsApp. Often forwarded the link by someone on their team.

## Job of this surface
Persuade mode. The visitor should (1) be impressed within 10 seconds ("these people are the real deal AND this is not scary"), (2) get one honest taste of what a workshop teaches, (3) tap the single CTA: WhatsApp us to book a half-day. No forms, no pricing on the page.

## Position
The magic that is missing in the local market, delivered by people who grew up with the tools. Professional, gamified, never gambling. Fast, modern, designer-grade, large and clear. Not too serious or restrained like most tech-training companies; the friendliness lives in the words, not in silly visuals.

## Brand commitments (must preserve)
- Logo: "kadabra" set in Gabarito Black, squared, counters cut with chevron notches, a playing card with a four-point sparkle to its left. The logo is the only place for Black/900. Site titles: Gabarito 800, never 900. Gabarito is for the logo and titles only.
- Leitmotifs: the four playing-card suits (custom SVGs), arrows/chevrons from the logo, movement, a magic wand, the sparkle. Sharp, serious magic.
- Playing cards themselves appear rarely, only for very important vertical-friendly moments (a confirmation card, a single question). No deck, no card backs, no casino, no Balatro.
- Palette (from the founder's mockup): felt green (deep, desaturated, main dark surface), white, carbon on cream for reading, one deep red for the CTA. Cream-heavy pages read as AI slop right now; lean green-heavy, but never a casino table.
- Motion: smooth (no mechanical steps), between Mistral and Locomotive in quantity, never dizzying. Big text that arrives, one looping hero animation (a grid of suits that morph), hover details, section reveals.
- Gamification: one puzzle on the whole page, and it makes a point. No confetti, no scolding, no levels, no collecting, no guilt.

## Platform
web

## Stack
Plain static HTML/CSS/JS per prototype, Google Fonts, no framework. Deploy: Cloudflare Pages.

## Language
English. Costa Rican examples and facts.
