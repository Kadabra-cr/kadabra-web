## Packaging patterns (6-8 bullets)

- Half-day vs full-day is the base unit almost everywhere abroad (BlueShore: $999 half / $1,499 full); Costa Rica private academies skip this and sell by course/module instead.
- Small-group flat fee is common abroad ("up to 5 trainees" at BlueShore, "10-25 participants" at Pertama) rather than strict per-seat pricing — the flat fee implicitly caps headcount.
- Cohort platforms (Maven) use genuine per-seat pricing with volume discounts (20%/25% off at higher seat counts) — this is the exception, not the rule, among boutique in-house sellers.
- Membership/retainer tier exists at the top end (Reforge team plans ~$10k/yr) rather than one-off workshops — positions AI/skills training as ongoing subscription, not event.
- "Multi-session program" or rollout tier appears as a vague, non-priced "call for pricing" step above the single workshop (BlueShore's Multi-Session Program), suggesting a pilot-then-scale sales motion even when not explicit.
- Costa Rican university/chamber offerings package by duration-and-credential (INA 40h certificate, TEC 24-week specialization) rather than by workshop event — closer to formal education than corporate training.
- Free/subsidized entry point is distinctive to Costa Rica (INA, Fidélitas, USAM courses are free or state-subsidized), which boutique paid consultancies abroad don't offer.
- Tool-specific framing (Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT) is used as a packaging hook both in Costa Rica (ia.co.cr's "Copilot for Enterprises") and abroad (General Assembly's Copilot-tailored workshops).

## CTA patterns (4-5 bullets)

- Costa Rica strongly favors WhatsApp as a direct contact channel alongside or instead of forms (TEC lists a WhatsApp number for the AI specialization; several consultancies like PorContar/Moulanier sell WhatsApp-based sales/quoting itself).
- Spanish-language CTAs cluster around "cotizar," "reservar espacio," "inscribirse," and "solicite más información" rather than a single universal verb — e.g. ia.co.cr's "ADQUIRIR CURSO & RESERVAR ESPACIO" combines purchase + seat-reservation in one button.
- Boutique English-language sellers use a two-CTA pattern: a direct "Book This Workshop" transactional button plus a softer "Talk to Us About Your Team" / "Request a Quote" for custom scoping (BlueShore.AI).
- Big names (General Assembly, Reforge) push toward a sales-contact/demo form rather than any visible self-serve price or checkout — pricing is gated behind a conversation.
- Cohort marketplaces (Maven) are the outlier with a fully self-serve "Join"/seat-selector checkout showing live price and discount tiers, no call required.
