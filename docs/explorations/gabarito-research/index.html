The origin story, in one paragraph

Commissioned 2017 for a Brazilian exam-prep platform. Started by Leandro Assis and Álvaro Franca, developed 2020 with Felipe Casaprima, reworked for open source in 2023 with Henrique Beier (Harbor Type). Named after the Brazilian Portuguese word for answer sheet — it was built so 17-year-olds cramming for the vestibular would find the page friendly rather than threatening, which is why it carries a full load of maths operators, set-theory symbols, roman numerals and scientific inferiors/superiors alongside a geometric sans that refuses to be cold.

5 · Reviews & pairings

  • Pimp my Type — Gabarito review The one serious critique. Verdict: geometric simplicity without geometric coldness (curved l terminal, charming question mark, multiple figure styles); a solid all-rounder for body text, UI and web. Limits worth knowing before you commit: no italics, and no light weight — so it gets heavy at very large display sizes. Pairs suggested: Niven (headings), Pitanga, Avona / Avona Serif, Roba.
  • MaxiBestOf — pairings & alternativesRecords Gabarito + DM Mono from real-world use. Names DIN Neuzeit Grotesk and Nexa as the nearest relatives.
  • FontPairings.net — Gabarito · FontForge.io — best pairings for GabaritoAlgorithmic pairing suggestions — Cormorant and Roboto Slab keep coming up. Treat as starting points, not gospel.
  • Fontpair · Font MatchGeneral tools: Font Match is the one for picking a system-font fallback so your FOUT doesn't reflow.

6 · Near-matches — NOT Gabarito

Kept deliberately separate. These are what people reach for when Gabarito doesn't fit — mostly because of the missing italics or the missing light weight. Useful as a fallback stack, a body-text partner, or a sanity check that Gabarito really is the distinctive choice.

  • DIN Neuzeit Grotesk · NexaMaxiBestOf's "fonts similar to Gabarito". Both commercial; closest structural cousins.
  • Capitana · Grato / GratimoPimp my Type's recommended swaps when you need real italics and a fuller family for body copy. Grato/Gratimo is also Henrique Beier / Harbor Type — the same hand that finished Gabarito, which is why they feel related.
  • Avona & Avona Serif · Roba · Niven · PitangaPimp my Type's companions rather than substitutes — Roba shares the geometric bones, Avona contrasts for headings.
  • Century Gothic · ITC Avant Garde GothicThe classical geometric-sans ancestors. Reference points for the genre, far colder than Gabarito — a good illustration of exactly what Gabarito is avoiding.

7 · What this means for a brand built on it

  • read Nobody famous has claimed it. No dominant association to fight — but also no reference brand to borrow credibility from. You'd be setting the precedent, not joining one.
  • read Every real use found sits in the same register: friendly, product-led, slightly playful, digital-first (SaaS landing page, productised service, designer portfolio, university poster). It has never been pushed luxury, editorial, or austere — that's an open lane, and also an untested one.
  • watch No italics, no light weight. Plan the hierarchy around weight and size contrast, not emphasis-by-slant. If long-form editorial copy is in your future, budget for a second family.
  • use The Black weight at display sizes is where it has the most personality; Regular/Medium is where it behaves like any competent UI sans.
  • bonus The maths/set-theory/scientific glyph load is unusual for a free sans. If your brand ever touches anything technical or educational, that's free range most competitors' fonts don't have.
