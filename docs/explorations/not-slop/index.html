not slop.

Websites and brands for serious businesses with less serious branding: AI, security, training and fintech companies that stay warm, weird and human while still selling to people who sign contracts.

Collected as reference for a lowercase AI-enablement brand (fast · safe · young · smart). This is inspiration, not brand strategy. Every link was checked live on 21 Sep 2026.

Same category tension as kadabra: a technical, slightly scary subject, made approachable.

granola

AI notepad that rebranded to look deliberately not like an AI company. The logo is a napkin-sketch "G", the typeface is made from a co-founder's handwriting, and the palette is earthy green on paper. It trended on X on launch day and had record downloads.

Brands and pages that make learning feel like a sprint rather than a semester.

  • The A–Z of AI (Google)Awwwards SOTD, Jun 2020. Bite-sized, one-letter-at-a-time AI explainers for non-experts, exactly the attention-span format. The live site now redirects to ai.google, so study it through the Awwwards page and archives.

05 · what the good ones share patterns, not prescriptions

One weird owned assetHandwriting (Granola), pixel cat (Mistral), hedgehog (PostHog), llama (Vanta). One thing only you have, used everywhere.
Warm colour in a cold categoryCoral in compliance, lime in AI, orange in LLMs. The category default is navy and purple gradients.
Personality in the wordsCleo, Oatly, Monzo and PostHog let the copy carry the joke while the layout stays calm and legible.
Let people touch itGandalf teaches AI security by letting you break it, and Dropbox's guidelines are playable. Show the hands-on approach instead of claiming it.
Deliberate imperfectionWobbly strokes, visible hand and texture. These are the cues AI output doesn't produce by default.
Candour as trustPublic handbooks, real prices, and "you'll hate us if…". Safe comes from honesty, not from a navy suit.

the slop checklist kept separate · what to steer away from

The recurring tells of generic AI-generated sites, as catalogued by people tracking it:

  • purple→blue gradientThe single most common tell.
  • Inter everywhereTypography is the fastest way out.
  • centred hero + three cardsThe default landing-page skeleton.
  • untouched shadcn / zincComponent defaults shipped as a brand.
  • reflexive glassmorphismBlur and glow standing in for ideas.
  • sparkle ✦ icons, "unlock", "supercharge"AI iconography and verbs that every competitor also uses.

Sources: 925 Studios · Venngage · SaaS UI · avoid-ai-design (repo). For a direct contrast in your own category, Section AI is the enterprise version of AI training: competent, corporate and large-scale. Useful for seeing what kadabra isn't.
