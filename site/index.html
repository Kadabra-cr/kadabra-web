AI without the smoke screen

The main workshop. Half a day, seven moments, your own work on the table.

  1. The smoke.

    What your team has been told AI does. We clear it in ten minutes, using your own examples.

  2. Your desk.

    Three real tasks from your team's week. Not ours. Yours.

  3. Hands on.

    Everyone at a keyboard or a phone, with the tools they already have. We bring our own tools for teaching, tailored to your team.

  4. Teaching magic.

    One task, done with AI, in front of everyone.

  5. Where it breaks.

    We make it fail on purpose, so you see it before a client does.

  6. House rules.

    What never leaves the building. Who checks what. Three lines, written together.

  7. Optional

    Next week.

    A one-page plan in your inbox: what to automate first, what to guard, what to ignore. Yours to use, no second session needed.

The trick, with receipts.

Four numbers explain why we teach the way we do. Each one links to where it came from. Read them, then bring us your team's week.

  1. 93%

    93% of Costa Rican tech companies save time on repetitive tasks with AI.

    Your competitors' tech teams already save time with AI. The tools work. That part is not in question.

    PROCOMER 2025 Caracterización del Sector TIC, via El Financiero, 2025

  2. 9%

    Only 9% have a formal AI policy.

    Almost nobody has written down what may leave the building, or who checks what. That is the part we fix in an afternoon.

    PROCOMER 2025 Caracterización del Sector TIC's Costa Rica, 2025

  3. 47%

    47% of employees have ever received any AI training.

    More than half of the people using AI at work were never shown how. Ask around your office.

    KPMG x Melbourne Business School, Trust, attitudes and use of AI: A global study 2025, 2025

  4. 34%

    Beginners who are taught get up to 34% more done.

    The biggest gains go to beginners who get taught. That is your team. That is the half-day.

    Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, NBER Working Paper 31161, Generative AI at Work, 2023
