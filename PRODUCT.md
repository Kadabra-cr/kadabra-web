# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Costa Rican companies, and the managers who decide on training for their teams. They meet kadabra on the website, on WhatsApp and on LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose

kadabra runs practical, interactive AI workshops for Costa Rican teams. Each workshop uses the team's own work and the tools it already uses. Success means a team that uses AI well: it knows what to hand to AI, what to check, and what to keep human.

## Positioning

"El truco no es la herramienta, es saber usarla." The tools already work; the risk is using them without being taught. kadabra teaches with the client's own files, shows AI failing on purpose, and writes the house rules together with the team. It sells no software and nothing to buy afterwards.

## Operating Context

Buyers book through WhatsApp. The website is the proof: real data from PROCOMER 2025, NBER 31161 and KPMG × Melbourne Business School 2025.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The main workshop is "Inteligencia Artificial sin humo". Its half-day format is secondary in outward messaging (founder, 2026-09-23).
- No prices, no invented customers, testimonials or benchmarks. Only the figures cited on the site.
- Studio name only: no faces.

## Brand Commitments

- Copy is Costa Rican professional Spanish, ustedeo only (never voseo or tuteo), in the voice of `src/copy.es.md`.
- Name written lowercase: kadabra. Second-edition logo and icon (`brand/kadabra-second-edition.svg`); the mark is never redrawn.
- Playing-card suits are the signature; card shapes are rare. The palette is felt green, carbon, cream, gold and a deep red.
- Fonts: Gabarito 800 for display, Hanken Grotesk for text.

## Evidence on Hand

- PROCOMER 2025: 93% of Costa Rican tech companies save time with AI; 9% have a formal AI policy.
- NBER 31161 (Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond 2023): up to +34% for beginners who are taught.
- KPMG × Melbourne Business School 2025: fewer than half of workers have had AI training.
- No testimonials, client logos or case studies exist yet. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Practice over slides: the team's own work is the material.
2. Show the risk honestly, not just the promise.
3. Plain claims with sources; no hype.
