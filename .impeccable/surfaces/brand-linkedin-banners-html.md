# LinkedIn banners and profile picture

Scope: three LinkedIn cover designs (personal 1584×396, company page 1128×191) and one profile picture (the icon alone). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: Costa Rican managers who come across kadabra on LinkedIn. Job: in one glance, see what kadabra is (practical AI workshops for teams) and where to look (the website). No WhatsApp number, no prices; the half-day format stays secondary.

Constraints: copy comes from the site's voice (ustedeo), editable in `brand/linkedin/copy.md`. LinkedIn covers the lower left with the photo or logo, and phones crop the sides. Keep key content right of centre-left, with generous margins.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each banner is one moment from the site, frozen at its best frame. It refuses the category default of a logo, a stock laptop photo and a list of services.
OWN-WORLD: felt green, carbon and cream fields; Gabarito 800 display type; smooth suits morphed from squares; one horizontal cream playing card with a red band; gold for the single emphasis; the second-edition lockup.
STORY: the visitor recognises a real question (can AI do this task?), a real risk (the smoke) or a real figure (93 vs 9), reads "Talleres de IA prácticos para equipos en Costa Rica" and the web address.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the right three fifths hold the headline at display scale plus one line of offer and the URL; the left two fifths hold the artefact (card, smoke, bars), clear of the photo area.
FORM: dealt structures 6 (the card), 4 (the smoke), 2 (93/9) from seed e9b3b3d0; all three built at the founder's request.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
